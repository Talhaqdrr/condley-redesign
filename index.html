Our building

Building financial confidence for over 80 years.

For decades Condley has called Abilene home. That's why we're proud to now call the historic Grant Building downtown our home base.

  • 1931Built as a local landmark that once buzzed with shoppers looking for goods at the W.T. Grant Merchandise Company.
  • 1963 onwardFollowing the store's move, the building had stints as law firms, an investment office and a restaurant, changes that erased much of its historic character.
  • TodayPartnering with local architects, Condley carefully restored original features like the limestone stairs and store-era lobby ceilings, a space that reflects our commitment to heritage and our penchant for thoughtful innovation.
